Official Canteen App — GGI Amritsar

Order food on campus, skip the queue.

Hunger Zone lets students and staff at Global Group of Institutes order from the campus canteen, Nescafe corner, Lipton stall and fruit counter — pay with UPI, track the order, and pick it up when it's ready.

How it works

From order to pickup in three steps.

01

Browse & order

Pick an outlet — Canteen, Nescafe, Lipton or Fruit Corner — and add items to your cart.

02

Pay with UPI

Checkout securely using any UPI app on your phone. No cash handling at the counter.

03

Track & collect

Get a live status update and a notification the moment your order is ready for pickup.

Who it's for

Hunger Zone is used by students and staff across the GGI Amritsar campus to order food during breaks without waiting in line at the counter. Admins at each outlet manage their own menu and fulfil orders as they come in.
