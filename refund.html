Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds are handled for orders placed through Hunger Zone.

Cancellation window

Orders can be cancelled free of charge before the outlet begins preparing them. Once an outlet marks an order as "in preparation," it can no longer be cancelled.

Refund eligibility

  • If an order is cancelled before preparation begins, the full amount is refunded.
  • If an outlet is unable to fulfil an order due to item unavailability, a full refund is issued automatically.
  • If a payment is deducted but the order does not reflect in the app, contact support with your transaction ID for verification and refund.

Refund timeline

Approved refunds are processed back to the original UPI account within 5–7 working days, depending on the payment gateway and bank processing times.

Non-refundable cases

Orders that have already been prepared and are ready for pickup are not eligible for a refund, except in cases of a genuine quality issue reported at the time of pickup.

For refund or payment issues, contact us at support@globalinstitutes.edu.in with your order ID and transaction reference.
