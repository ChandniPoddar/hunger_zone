Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Hunger Zone ("we", "our", "the app") is a food ordering application built for students and staff of Global Group of Institutes, Amritsar. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Name and phone number, used to create your account and verify orders.
  • Order history — items ordered, outlet, time, and order status.
  • Device information needed for push notifications about order status.

How we use your information

  • To process and fulfil your food orders at campus outlets.
  • To send order status updates and notifications.
  • To improve the app and resolve issues if you contact support.

Payments

Payments are processed through UPI and third-party payment gateways. We do not store your card, UPI PIN, or banking credentials on our servers — these are handled directly by the payment provider.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only with the relevant campus outlet (e.g. Canteen, Nescafe Corner) to fulfil your order, and with our payment gateway to process transactions.

Data retention

Order and account data is retained as long as your account is active, and may be retained for a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping and dispute resolution.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us using the details below.

Questions about this policy? Contact us at support@globalinstitutes.edu.in or call +91 99157 57932.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
